How I Work

I don't wait for the problem to become an emergency.

I'm not here to add another person for you to manage. I'm here to help you manage the operation. Don't just bring me the problem, bring me the context, I'll help move it toward a solution within scope.

U

Understand

Learn your workflow, priorities, expectations, and boundaries.

A

Anticipate

Look ahead and notice what may need attention next, before it's an emergency.

A

Act

Handle appropriate tasks within scope, nothing outside it without asking.

C

Communicate

Clear updates before you have to ask: what happened, what's next, what I need from you.

M

Manage Up

Summarize what matters, recommend next steps when appropriate, make decisions easier.

F

Follow Through

Keep open loops moving until completion, escalation, or a clear next action.

Situation, action, impact, recommendation, decision.

  • Situation: "The tenant reported a recurring leak."
  • Action: "I reviewed the maintenance history and contacted the vendor."
  • Impact: "This is the third report in two months, so it may need more than a temporary repair."
  • Recommendation: "I'd request an inspection first, so we have a clear diagnosis before deciding on the next repair."
  • Decision: "I'll proceed with the inspection once you approve."
Proactive vs Reactive

You shouldn't have to manage the person you hired to help manage the details.

Reactive
  • Waits for instructions
  • Waits for you to ask what's happening
  • Reports problems without context
  • Stops once the immediate task is done
Proactive
  • Understands the outcome you're after
  • Tracks open loops and communicates before you ask
  • Brings context, options, and a recommendation
  • Follows through and escalates when necessary

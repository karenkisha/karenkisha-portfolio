How I Work

I don't wait for the problem to become an emergency.

I'm not here to add another person for you to manage. I'm here to help you manage the operation. Don't just bring me the problem, bring me the context, I'll help move it toward a solution within scope.

U

Understand

Learn your workflow, priorities, expectations, and boundaries.

A

Anticipate

Look ahead and notice what may need attention next, before it's an emergency.

A

Act

Handle appropriate tasks within scope, nothing outside it without asking.

C

Communicate

Clear updates before you have to ask: what happened, what's next, what I need from you.

M

Manage Up

Summarize what matters, recommend next steps when appropriate, make decisions easier.

F

Follow Through

Keep open loops moving until completion, escalation, or a clear next action.

Situation, action, impact, recommendation, decision.

  • Situation: "The tenant reported a recurring leak."
  • Action: "I reviewed the maintenance history and contacted the vendor."
  • Impact: "This is the third report in two months, so it may need more than a temporary repair."
  • Recommendation: "I'd request an inspection first, so we have a clear diagnosis before deciding on the next repair."
  • Decision: "I'll proceed with the inspection once you approve."

04 · Decision Framework

How the status button got touched

Not just what it landed on.

Step 1
Verify the requirement

What does Maple Grove's written screening criteria actually require? Minimum 2.5x rent-to-income, no eviction history, no criminal history.

Step 2
Verify the applicant data

What income and screening information is actually on file for Carlos Ramirez? $4,800/mo verified income, employer confirmed, background check clear.

Step 3
Calculate
$4,800 ÷ $2,195 = 2.19x
Step 4
Compare against the bar
Required 2.5x ($5,487.50/mo), Actual 2.19x ($4,800/mo), short by $687.50/mo
Step 5
Check the remaining criteria

Eviction history and background screening both pass clean, so the decision comes down to the one criterion that didn't.

Step 6
Apply the policy consistently

The income requirement is the least negotiable of the three. Two of three passing doesn't average out to a qualifying application; it fails.

Step 7
Document and route the outcome

Record the objective reason, follow the applicant-notification process, and make sure no lease gets generated for an application that didn't qualify.

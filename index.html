How I Work

I don't forward messages. I close loops.

Every request goes through the same four moves, whether it's a single tenant question or a maintenance issue that needs a vendor.

U

Understand

I get the full picture before acting, what happened, who's involved, and what actually needs to happen next.

C

Coordinate

I bring in the right person, whether that's a tenant, a vendor, or you, and keep the thread moving instead of stalling.

F

Follow Through

I check back instead of assuming it's handled. If something stalls, I know before you have to ask.

X

Close the Loop

Every open item gets a confirmed resolution and shows up in your report, so nothing lives in limbo.

Property Manager Decision Brief

Applied Judgment
[ Decision brief document: applicant scenario, reasoning, and final call ]
Situation

An applicant met most, but not all, of the standard leasing criteria, and needed a real decision, not just a policy lookup.

Reasoning

Weighing income ratio, screening flags, and risk against what the policy actually allows for a conditional approval.

Decision

Conditionally approved, with an increased security deposit to offset the added risk.
